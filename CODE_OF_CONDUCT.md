# Code of conduct

Help keep the Preen community welcoming and useful. Be respectful and
constructive; welcome different levels of experience, give actionable feedback,
and focus disagreement on ideas and work rather than personal attacks.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and sharing someone
else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, limit participation
or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Do not post
sensitive incident details publicly. Check the
[maintainer profile](https://github.com/mdadeel) for an available private
contact method; if none is listed, use a minimal
[public issue](https://github.com/mdadeel/Preen/issues/new) only to request a
private contact, without identifying details.

For security reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. GitHub's own
community rules also apply to activity hosted on GitHub.
